How It Works

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about LocalJSON.

Is my JSON data sent to a server?
No. LocalJSON runs 100% in your browser. Your data never leaves your device — no uploads, no tracking, no server processing.
Does LocalJSON work offline?
Yes. Once loaded, LocalJSON works completely offline. The Pro Offline Version is a standalone HTML file that requires no internet connection at all.
What is the free tier limit?
Free tier allows 3 actions per day (format, map, or export = 1 action each). Counter resets at midnight local time.
How do I upgrade to Pro?
Purchase a license key (format: LJSON-PRO-XXXXXXXX) from our store, then enter it in the app. Pro unlocks unlimited actions permanently.
Can I convert JSON to CSV?
Yes. Map your JSON array columns, then export to CSV (UTF-8 with BOM) for Excel compatibility.
What browsers are supported?
Any modern browser with ES2020 support: Chrome 80+, Firefox 75+, Safari 14+, Edge 80+.
